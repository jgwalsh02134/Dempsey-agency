What we do

Services

Four services cover a Dempsey engagement: planning and buying, publisher placements, creative direction for those placements, and the reporting that follows. Scope is set in the proposal for a specific campaign.

Planning & buying

Strategic Media Planning & Buying

We build a multi-platform plan around the markets you need and the publisher environments that fit the message. National reach is the footprint. Local precision is which markets get a tighter recommendation.

Buying follows that plan so placements are not a pile of unrelated units. Efficiency here means a clearer campaign direction: what is in the buy, what is out, and why.

Placements

Premium Omnichannel Placements

Placements run in trusted publisher environments:

  • Print
  • Digital editions
  • Publisher websites
  • Newsletters

The mix depends on the plan. This site does not publish a rate card or a standing audience total. Those figures belong in the proposal for the markets and flight you actually need.

AdSell.ai, an affiliated company, is a marketplace for local print and digital community media. It is a related path to local placement, not an automatic part of every Dempsey buy.

Creative

Creative Strategy

Creative strategy is campaign direction and messaging for high-trust media. The message is written for the environment it will run in, whether that is a print page, a digital edition, a publisher site, or a newsletter.

The point is fit. A unit made for one environment is not assumed to work, unchanged, in the others on the plan.

Reporting

Performance Analytics & Optimization

Reporting covers what ran, where it ran, and how it compared with the plan. The inputs are campaign performance and publisher data. Optimization uses that same information while the flight is still movable.

We do not advertise a house return or a benchmark on this website. Performance is discussed against the campaign you commissioned.
