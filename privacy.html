Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This policy describes how Dempsey Agency, LLC ("Dempsey Agency," "we," "us") handles information in connection with the public website at dempsey.agency. It does not cover the client portal at portal.dempsey.agency, the team intranet, or the websites of affiliated companies. Those properties have their own practices.

Who we are

Information you give us

Email. If you write to info@dempsey.agency, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you include. We use that information to respond and to discuss media planning, placements, or related services.

Account requests. The pages at /request-access and /request-account ask for your name, email address, company, and an optional message. We use that information to review and respond to a request for a client portal account. Submissions are sent to our application service at api.dempsey.agency.

Account activation. If you open an invitation at /activate-account, we receive the invitation token in the link, the email address and organization tied to that invitation, an optional name, and the password you choose. We use this only to create and secure your client portal account. Do not forward invitation links.

We do not ask for payment card numbers on this website.

Information collected automatically

The marketing pages are static. They do not include advertising pixels or a first-party analytics script, and we do not sell personal information.

When you load a page, our host (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare describes that processing in its own privacy policy.

Pages load fonts from Adobe Fonts (use.typekit.net). Adobe may receive your IP address and browser details when a font file is requested. See Adobe's privacy policy for that service.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • reply to inquiries and account requests
  • set up a client portal account you were invited to activate
  • operate, secure, and maintain this website
  • meet legal obligations

We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share information with

We share information with service providers that host the site, deliver email, or run the account system, and only as needed for those tasks. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Dempsey Agency, our clients, or the public.

Vision Data (vdata.com) and AdSell.ai (adsell.ai) are affiliated companies with their own websites. Following those links leaves dempsey.agency. This policy does not apply to them.

Retention

We keep inquiry and account-request messages for as long as needed to respond, keep a record of the business conversation, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Invitation tokens are used to activate an account; they are not a marketing list. Passwords are stored by the account system in a protected form and are not kept in this website.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold from your emails or account requests, and you may ask us to correct or delete it, subject to records we are required to keep. Email info@dempsey.agency with the subject line "Privacy request."

You can control cookies in your browser. This site does not set advertising cookies. Adobe Fonts and Cloudflare may set cookies or similar technical identifiers while delivering fonts and the site.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Pages are served over HTTPS. We rely on our host and application providers to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top of this page will change when we do. The current version is always posted at dempsey.agency/privacy.
