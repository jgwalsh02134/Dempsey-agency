Dempsey Agency, LLC

About

Dempsey Agency is an independent media agency in New York's Capital Region. We plan and buy premium publisher placements for advertisers who need national reach and local precision.

Where we work

Rensselaer, New York

The company is Dempsey Agency, LLC, at 1377 Third Street, Rensselaer, NY 12144. Campaigns can be national in scope. Placement decisions are made market by market, including markets close to this office and markets far from it.

The work is media planning and buying: strategy, publisher environments, creative direction for those environments, and reporting once a campaign is running.

Who it's for

Advertisers working across markets

The useful client is an advertiser, or the team marketing for one, that needs a plan covering more than one market and placements in premium publisher environments: print, digital editions, websites, and newsletters.

If the job is only a client-portal login, use the client portal. New planning conversations start on the contact page.

Affiliated companies

Related, and still separate

Two affiliated companies sit next to the agency. They have their own sites, and a Dempsey engagement is not automatically work on those sites.

AdSell.ai is a marketplace for reaching local communities through print and digital local media. That lines up with the local-precision side of our placement work. It does not replace a Dempsey media plan, and buying through Dempsey is not the same thing as listing a campaign on AdSell.

Vision Data is at the same Rensselaer address. Its public description is strong accounting driving marketing. It is a separate business. It is not the publisher plan, and this site does not claim Vision Data products as Dempsey deliverables.

Dempsey Agency, LLC remains an independent business entity. The national reach and local precision in our work come from how a plan is built and where it is placed.
