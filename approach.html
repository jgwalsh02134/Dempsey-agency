How we work

Approach

An engagement moves from the markets you need to a plan, then into publisher environments, then into reporting. These six steps are the work. They are not a promise of a particular result.

01

Discover

We start with the geography, the timing, and the job the media has to do. A useful brief names the markets, the audience those markets imply, and what a placement is supposed to accomplish. The contact form is built for that conversation.

02

Plan

Strategic media planning turns that brief into a multi-platform recommendation: where scale matters, where a local market needs a tighter list, and which publisher environments belong in the mix. Buying follows the plan so the campaign has a direction.

03

Shape

Creative strategy fits the message to the environment. A print page, a digital edition, a publisher website, and a newsletter do not carry the same unit. Direction is set before placement, so the creative is made for those high-trust settings.

04

Place

Buying and placement put the plan into market. The environments we work in are print, digital editions, websites, and newsletters. National reach is the footprint of the plan. Local precision is which publishers and markets are actually on the order.

05

Optimize

Once a campaign is in market, changes follow campaign performance and publisher data. Optimization is part of the engagement, not a separate product. What can move depends on the placements and the flight.

06

Report

Reporting says what ran, where it ran, and how it performed against the plan. It uses the same campaign and publisher information that guides optimization. We do not publish a standard return figure on this site. Results belong to the specific campaign.
